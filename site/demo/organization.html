Organization

Every record has a home, a shape, and one verb that writes it.

This is the part of BOSS that does the filing so the founder never has to. Nothing lives in the chat: what you say becomes a record in a folder, in a fixed shape, with an id and a status line — and the playbook, the design space and the board are reads of those folders, never documents anyone maintains. Change the work and the pages change; there is nothing else to update.

The four rules it keeps

One verb, one shape

A persona is always six fields and a ledger; a decision always carries a falsifier with a date; evidence is always one signal, graded. The shape is what lets a page be a read instead of a rewrite.

the shipped skills' templates

The status line is the truth

captured · ready · building · shipped · deferred · dropped — a word on the record's own frontmatter. The board reads it; nobody drags a card. A parked idea carries its re-open trigger in the same line.
