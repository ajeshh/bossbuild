2 · Product

What: One tap when a carer calls in sick.

What it is today — the current shape, in the founder's words

The best articulation so far.

  • What: One tap when a carer calls in sick. The schedule stays a spreadsheet; Kettlewick only does the cover.
  • Who it's for: owner-operators with 3 to 15 carers who do the Monday schedule by hand.
  • Why now: owners moved the schedule to their phones in 2024–25; the cover call didn't follow.
  • Smallest version that proves it: a text goes out to the three carers who can take the visit; the first yes fills it; the owner sees it filled.

Trust — what you collect, in plain terms

We hold the schedule you give us and the phone numbers on it; nothing else. Carers' locations are never read. Nothing is sold, shared or used to train anything. Delete your agency and it is gone in a day, texts included. To ask about your data, text the number you already have.
