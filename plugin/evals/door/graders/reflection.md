The response says the founder's idea BACK to them before doing anything else, in the founder's own words, not smoothed into startup language. A passing response:

- names the specific people (dog walkers, Leeds), the specific event (a last-minute cancellation / a free slot another walker can pick up), and the money (about £5 a month) — at least three of these four, in words close to the founder's own;
- states the smallest version that would prove the idea (a way for walkers to post and claim a slot; no more than that);
- if it read anything two ways (for example who pays, or whether "nearby" is a radius), it says which way it read it rather than asking an open question.

A response FAILS if it renames the idea into a pitch ("a marketplace platform for pet-care professionals"), asks the founder to describe the idea again, launches into a stack recommendation, or lists features the founder never mentioned.
