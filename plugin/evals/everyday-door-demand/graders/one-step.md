The response names ONE next step, and that step is a conversation with a real person about the problem (or, failing a person to talk to, a cheap demand test) — because the project on disk has an idea and no evidence, and that is the smallest real step from there. A passing response:

- says the step in plain words a first-time founder would understand ("talk to one dog walker who has lost money to a cancellation"; "put up a page that says what it does and see who leaves an email");
- asks at most ONE question, or none;
- does not re-ask what the idea is — the idea is in docs/ideas and the response shows it read it (dog walkers, Leeds, cancellations, or the £5 — at least one of these in its own words);
- stops there, or does the step (drafts the questions for the conversation, or hands off to it).

A response FAILS if it proposes filling in a canvas or writing a plan first, proposes building anything, gives a tour of BOSS or lists what BOSS can do, asks three or more questions, or asks the founder to describe the idea again.
