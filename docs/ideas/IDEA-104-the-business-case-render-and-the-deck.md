  Holes render as holes; nothing is invented to fill a cell.
- **Deck** — rung 4. The refusal was amended from "never a deck" to "never a deck BOSS has to invent

- **Shareable link** and **investor update** — both gated on n>0; do not build on imagination.

Layer 3 (holes render never fill · an evidence ledger on the whole profile · freshness · the
"not yet" default survives the render · at n=0 the render is a conscience moment) applies to every
rung and is already the discipline the one-pager ships with.

is the whole profile a founder can share.
