# IDEA-069 — What is this meant to outlast?

## The gap

[[DEC-012]] says BOSS holds both Silicon Valley and the ancient ways. The Silicon Valley half is
thoroughly mechanised. The ancient half is **half-mechanised, and the missing half is time.**

Audit of every time-shaped thing BOSS asks a founder:

| Mechanism | What it asks | Direction |
|---|---|---|
| `/sunset` | the honest post-mortem, the harvest, the clean close | **ending** |
| Business Model, 2nd branch ([[DEC-009]]) | who else could carry it · what if you're hit by a bus · what would make you stop | **ending** |
| `PRINCIPLES.md` (the why) | *"decide faster, including quitting faster"* (Camuffo) | **ending** |
| `drift` moment | is this work testing the named bet | **now** |
| `/health`, `/measure` | retention, activation, the Month-3 cohort | **now** |
| Principle 1 (extract UP) | does this pattern outlive this project | **lasting** — but serves BOSS, not the founder |
| `/practice` | shared craft, attributed, staleness-aware | **lasting** — team-scoped, not project-scoped |

**Every question pointed at the founder's project is either *now* or *how it ends*.** The two
mechanisms that genuinely carry a long horizon (Principle 1, `/practice`) point at *BOSS's* commons
and the team's craft — not at the thing being built.

Succession is not the same question. *"Who carries this after you"* is about **not dying**.
*"What is this meant to outlast"* is about **what it is for**. A founder can answer the first with
"nobody, and I'll archive it" and still be building something intended to matter for twenty years.

## Why it might matter

- It is the one question that separates *shipping* from *stewardship*, and BOSS currently only asks
  the former. A tool that helps someone build a co-op, a commons, or an institution and never asks
  what horizon they're building on is holding one way while claiming two — **[[DEC-012]] falsifier #2,
  exactly.**
- It is cheap if it exists at all: it is one question, and the natural home is the canvas's
  **Principles** cell (*"the non-negotiables you'll hold even when it's costly"*) or a second line in
  the sustainability branch — **composition, not a new cell**, per [[EVID-001]].
- The honest answer is often *"I don't know yet"* or *"a year"* and **both must stay complete
  answers** — the same discipline as *"very little, and here's why"* (v0.201.0) and *"a 'no' is a real
  answer and the common one"* (the six shape questions).

## Why it is NOT built

**n=0.** No founder has asked to be asked this. It is derived from a charter clause and an audit, not
from a signal — which is the exact provenance [[DEC-009]] and [[DEC-011]] both refused to build on.
Two of the last three decisions in this repo turned on *"do not widen at n=0,"* and this idea would be
a poor place to break the streak.

**And it carries a real risk of being ceremony.** *"What is this meant to outlast?"* asked of someone
capturing their first idea is exactly the premature-ceremony failure Principle 2 exists to prevent.
If it is ever built it belongs late — V1 or Scale — or attached to a moment where the founder has
already shown they're building to last.

## Re-open triggers

- A founder answers the sustainability branch in a way that reaches for a horizon and has nowhere to
  put it.
- [[DEC-012]] hits `revisit_by` (2026-11-21) with falsifier #2 still open — the ancient half still
  has no question a founder is ever asked.
- A `/canvas` re-aim reaches the Principles cell and finds it thinner than the charter now claims.
