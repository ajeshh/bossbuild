# IDEA-041 — Distribution as a conscience leg (not just a mentor)

> **RESOLVED + voicing slice SHIPPED (v0.93.0, 2026-06-21).** Decided the leg-vs-mentor question with Ajesh:
> **neither pole — a third answer.** Not an auto-firing hook (its predicate needs the reachability detection
> parked in FEAT-024 slice 3; building it = the over-fire growth-hacking nag IDEA-019 guards against), and not
> mentor-only (the asymmetry stays). Instead a **single demand voicing wired into `/ship`** at the live moment
> (*"who's the first real user, and how do they hit this?"*) — leg-like (fires in the flow) without a hook
> (rides the deliberate `/ship` invocation → zero over-fire), situation-not-person, points at `mentor-gtm`,
> never a gate. **The full distribution *hook* stays deferred** — earned only if a reliable
> reachable-but-undiscovered predicate ever exists (shares FEAT-024 slice 3's parked substrate question).

## The asymmetry (the actual finding)

[`PRINCIPLES.md`](../../PRINCIPLES.md) names a **path to distribution** as a leg of *real business-value app*
— co-equal with real pain, real workflow, real economics. But look at what the conscience actually **voices**:

| Real-value leg | Has a firing moment? |
|---|---|
| Real pain / proves-an-assumption | ✅ moment #1 "what does this prove?" |
| Real economics / willingness-to-pay | ✅ rides moment #1 + `mentor-business` |
| Reliability | ✅ `/evals` / `/red-team` |
| **Path to distribution** | ❌ **no moment — content you go visit (`mentor-gtm`), not a moment that interrupts** |

So *"will anyone pay?"* gets voiced in the flow of building; *"will anyone ever find it?"* does not. A founder
can build, prove pain, even ship reachable (→ [[FEAT-024]]) and the conscience never once asks about
discovery. **That's the gap — and it's on-ethos, because distribution is already in the spine; it's just
under-voiced.**

## The question to decide (not yet answered)

**Should distribution be a conscience *leg* (a moment that fires in the flow), or stay a *mentor* you
consult?** `mentor-gtm` exists (L1/MVP) and is good — but it's a door you choose to open, not the
conscience naming a cost at the right moment. The reframe: graduate distribution from *content* to *moment*.

## The trap (why this is hard, not just "add a marketing nudge")

- **Growth-hacking is the exact cohort BOSS must not repel.** [[IDEA-019]] already settled the humane line
  the hard way: **describe the situation, never the person**; no shame-funnel; the indie-hacker /
  anti-growth-hacking cohort flees a nag. A clumsy "have you posted on Product Hunt?" moment is precisely the
  dark-pattern BOSS warns against. Any distribution voicing inherits `conscience-voicing.md` + `harm-taxonomy.md`.
- **Detectability (the over-fire question).** What's the honest predicate? Candidate: a **reachable, shipped
  MVP `FEAT` with no first-user / no distribution path recorded** → *"who's the first real user, and how do
  they find this?"* once. This is the same careful hook-predicate problem as [[FEAT-024]] slice 3 — voicing
  before hook.
- **Polishing the pitch ≠ progress on demand.** The recurring caution ([[IDEA-019]], [[IDEA-036]]):
  distribution-thinking can become *more building-around-the-n=0-risk* instead of ending it.

## Smallest honest slice (when earned)

A single **distribution-readiness voicing** at the reachable-but-undiscovered moment — *"who's the first
real user, and how do they find it?"* — borrowing [[FEAT-024]]'s reachability predicate, governed by
[[IDEA-019]]'s situation-not-person rule, never a gate. **Decide the leg-vs-mentor question with Ajesh
before building.**

  reachable → discoverable. Build FEAT-024 first; this is its sequel.
