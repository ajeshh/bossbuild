# IDEA-110 — The full showcase on oyeboss

## The idea

The site describes BOSS. Nothing on it *shows* BOSS running on a project from the first `boss new`
to a playbook a founder could hand to a room. The prototype built for IDEA-106 (a fictional
home-care venture, Tidewell) is the first artifact that made all the pieces visible at once — the
canvas, the personas, the rivals, the evidence ladder, the decisions, the brand, the design page a
peer session built alongside it — and Ajesh's read was that *this* is what repositioning BOSS
needs: one complete worked example, on the site, with every piece in it.

## What "all the pieces" means — a list, not a design

- **The setup** — `boss new`, the scaffold, `/welcome` → `/boss`, what the tree looks like on day
  one and what it earned by day thirty (`earned` groups, `boss status`).
- **The records** — the IDEA doc with its capture log, the canvas, `docs/personas/`,
  `docs/competition/`, `docs/evidence/` (fictional — no real person's words), `DEC`s, the devlog.
- **The renders** — `boss board --html`, `boss playbook` (every chapter, the deck, a VC cut), the
  Design page (the sibling artifact), `boss help --html`.
- **The conscience** — the moments that fired along the way, quoted, with what the founder did.
- **The mentors** — one `/consult`, one `mentor-capital` *not yet* sentence.

## The line it must respect

The example is **fictional and says so on every page** (no photo of a real person, no quote nobody
said — the same rules the playbook enforces), and every page is **generated by the real
renderers from a real throwaway project's files**, never hand-written to look like output. A demo
that is prettier than what `boss` produces is a lie the first install exposes.

## Open

- Is the venture Tidewell (already drawn, six rounds of reactions) or a second one that shows a
  different cohort? Tidewell is `non-tech-founder`; the site's other reader is `eng-builder`.
- Where does it live — `oyeboss.build/demo`, or a linked family of artifacts like the prototype?
- "Reposition" is Ajesh's word and IDEA-109's territory (the incubator-hires-me framing); this
  record holds the *showcase*, not the positioning. Read both before doing either.
