# IDEA-040 — The V1→Scale mode rung (org/scaling discipline)

> **Why this is its own idea, not an open thread of FEAT-023.** FEAT-023's buildable, solo-applicable
> scope was the build-*process* + architecture practices (threads 1–2 — `git-workflow`, `scalable-
> architecture`); both shipped (v0.88.0, v0.89.0) and that FEAT is **shipped**. This — the org/scaling
> rung of the mode ladder — is a *different concern at a different altitude* (the org, not the repo), it's
> **not solo-applicable**, and it's gated on a trigger that doesn't exist yet. Holding it as a "building"
> thread would have made FEAT-023 a zombie-open feature (the exact self-fooling BOSS exists to prevent —
> status frontmatter is truth, and the board projects it). So it lives here, correctly classified as a
> captured idea waiting on its trigger. It **promotes to a FEAT (via `/spec`) when the trigger fires** —
> not before.

## ⏸ DEFERRED — DO NOT REHASH (decided 2026-06-20)

The decision *not to build this yet* is **settled** — don't re-litigate it each session.

- **The ONLY thing that re-opens this:** a real project reaches the **V1 stage** (`boss unlock v1`) and
  hits an actual coordination/handoff symptom — a live dogfood, not a hypothetical. Until that trigger
  fires, building this rung is premature ceremony (Principle #2): there is no project at this altitude to
  design against, the canvas deprioritizes Scale authoring, and the n=0-real-users risk dominates.
- **Alternate trigger:** if a *founding-team* project hits the give-away-your-Legos handoff *before* a solo
  project hits V1, that handoff is the entry point — but build **only the conscience moment** there (it's
  [[FEAT-021]] slice-6-adjacent) and leave the rest of the rung deferred.
- **When the trigger fires:** the spec below is *researched, ready starting material* — author the rung
  from it; do **not** re-research or re-debate the deferral.

## The spine (B6 research — ready to build on)

The unauthored top rung of the mode ladder, built on a real spine:

- **Roles now (n=2–5):** a **DRI per decision** (ownership without hierarchy — the same primitive
  `/decide`'s who-decided field already carries in [[FEAT-021]]) + a **FACe** (Function Accountability
  Chart — founders' names repeat; *a name appearing too many times = the signal to hire/specialize*; drop
  the quarterly ceremony).
- **PMF gates specialization, not headcount** (pre-PMF bet on range; T-shaped is the bridge).
- **"Give away your Legos"** (Molly Graham) — a **conscience moment at the first real handoff** (the
  bottleneck is the founder's *emotional* resistance, not the org chart). This is the most distinctive,
  build-first piece, and the one with an alternate (teams) trigger.
- **Structure = founders' intuition written down** (DRI + operating principles + a how-we-operate charter —
  scales *judgment*, not headcount; BOSS already does the decision+why habit: RESUME/devlog/FEAT/`DEC`).
- **Stage breakpoints tied to *coordination symptoms*, not headcounts** (~first-manager / first-process /
  first-org-chart — Dunbar-applied, directional). **AI flattens the curve** (teams stay smaller longer; tie
  transitions to coordination *load*) — adopt as a hypothesis the ladder absorbs.
- **Reject the solo-unicorn / $1B-one-person forecasts** (interested-party hype; the irreducible human
  share is novel judgment — the Novelty Bottleneck). The ladder absorbs the "AI flattens the curve"
  direction *without* the multipliers.
