# IDEA-167 — The Floor

The shop floor, where things get made. For a side project, a tool, a commons — anything that wants
BOSS's building habits without being asked about its riskiest assumption.

## What's on the floor (draft)

- Everything on the Desk ([[IDEA-166]])
- Agents: coder, planner, tester, designer, mentor-architect, prompt-coach
- Skills: `/spec`, `/smoke`, `/prototype`, `/design-review`, `/ship`, `/extract`, `/revalidate`;
  with an AI feature, `/evals`, `/red-team`, `/ai-cost`, `/ai-failure-states`

Not on the floor: product-lead (deciding what is *worth* building is a venture question), the
conscience (it reads the venture), the mentors, canvas, evidence, money. `/roadmap` and `/sunset` sit
on the line — decide each.
