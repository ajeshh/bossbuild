# IDEA-171 — Rooms side by side at the top

Ajesh, 2026-10-08: *"this program solves alot of capitalistic vs non-capitalistic, we can also invent
new floors for different types of groups of people and package it or make the pieces more modular for
packaging that best align to it."* Decision: **capture now, build later.**

## The shape

**Vertical is how much BOSS; horizontal is what it's for.** The floors (DEC-024) are one cumulative
ladder, each floor more BOSS than the one below. A co-op is not more or less than a startup, only
different, so a new kind of group is **not a new floor**: putting it on the ladder would rank purposes.
Instead, the top floor holds rooms side by side:

| Level 5, side by side | For | Holds |
|---|---|---|
| **Boardroom** | a venture | today's BOSS: mentors, canvas, evidence, money |
| **Council room** (name open) | a co-op, a member-owned group | governance, decision rights, member shares |
| **Commons** (name open) | open source, shared resources | maintainers, contributors, succession, funding without selling |

**A room is a package: a named list of parts.** IDEA-163 is turning BOSS into parts and DEC-024 makes a
floor a list of them, so a room is one more list (the Studio's parts plus the room's own). The
modular packaging needs no new mechanism; it falls out of the parts work.

## Why deferred

- **Nothing exists yet to fill a non-venture room.** The support it needs (maintainer burnout,
  succession, contributor pipeline, member governance) is IDEA-067 rung 3, deferred by DEC-009 at n=0.
  Writing it before someone needs it is building on imagination (the EVID-001 lesson).
- **DEC-011 §5 says no commons mode.** Building a Commons room supersedes that; it gets its own DEC
  when the trigger fires.

## Rules for when it's built

- **Name a room by what happens inside it, never by ideology.** "Council room", "Commons" describe the
  work; a room named for a politics sorts people by belief (the humane lens would flag it).
- **No room is the default top.** Entering level 5 asks which room, the way the door asks which floor.

## Re-open trigger

The first real co-op or commons founder (an EVID, not a persona), **or** BOSS deciding to run itself as
a commons. BOSS is MIT-licensed, a commons with a venture half: its own needs may be the first evidence.
