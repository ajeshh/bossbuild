# `/scout humane` — keep the humane lens current

BOSS's own domain. Was `/humane-refresh`. The seed: *"keep checking frequently for new [dark
patterns] since it keeps changing"* (Ajesh, 2026-06-21, IDEA-042). A frozen humane lens is the one
failure BOSS can't afford (PRINCIPLE #6).

## Two kinds, and the second is why the catalog once missed cookie banners for three sweeps

- **What's new** (the default) — sweep the watchlist's standing query `since last_refresh`, biased by
  an event. Finds the *edge*.
- **`coverage`** — walk the catalog's shape × surface matrix and ask what is thin or empty. Finds the
  hole in the *middle*. Start from `npm run check:patterns` (counts, thin surfaces, unrouted surfaces,
  the dose ceiling); then ask a founder's question per shape — *what does someone building this
  actually ship that could deceive?*; look for the surface nobody has named (a new modality, a new
  actor, a new relationship). **Subtract here too:** a row that never fires, a surface no shape
  reaches, a candidate unvetted for two sweeps. Run it at least every other cadence sweep, and after
  adding a product shape.

## The domain's own steps

1. **Diff into four buckets** — already covered (cite the row) · sharpens · genuinely new · 🔴 now
   wrong (a pattern the field has redefined, a rule vacated). Quote the row a finding duplicates.
2. **Judge through `/vet`**, one claim per verdict, default NO; `mentor-humane` can override.
3. **Route** — a new row in `library/deceptive-patterns.json` always carries the **honest
   alternative**, never the cost without the fix; a harm axis in `harm-taxonomy.md` if it is new; a
   new shape or surface is the rarer, higher-value edit. `/red-team --humane` is derived from the
   catalog, so a row on an existing surface needs no skill edit.
4. **Stamp** `last_refresh` and `next_review` on the watchlist, add a refresh-log row, prune or add
   one tap, and write the session record.

A scheduled sweep (quarterly) **proposes a PR, never commits.** Event triggers: a new frontier model,
a new major tool or platform, a new regulation or ruling.
