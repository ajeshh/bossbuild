# RVW-055 — cognitive debt / craft erosion from AI offloading (MIT "Your Brain on ChatGPT")

## The claim
- **Source:** MIT Media Lab, *Your Brain on ChatGPT* (arXiv 2506.08872, Jun 2025 — EEG shows weaker neural connectivity in the LLM group, persisting after AI removed; 78% couldn't quote their own just-written essay) + Microsoft/CMU critical-thinking-atrophy survey.
- **Core assertion:** Offloading thinking to AI compounds into skill/judgment erosion; founders should stay "in the loop" on the reasoning, not just the output.

## Rubric
| # | Question | Finding |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Contradicts a PRINCIPLE? | No. |
| 2 | Evidence grade | **Soft.** MIT study is a preprint, n=54 (18 in the key crossover), essay-writing not building, with a published rebuttal (arXiv 2601.00856). Viral but methodologically thin. |
| 3 | Duplicate or sharpen? | Adjacent to the competence-gate ([[RVW-039]]) and 70% problem ([[RVW-053]]), which are better-evidenced. |
| 4 | Who serves / harms? | Real concern for all cohorts — but **easy to sound preachy**, which fights BOSS's voice (assume intelligence; the seasoned hand doesn't lecture). |
| 5 | Cost / ceremony | A conscience line would be cheap — but on thin evidence it risks moralizing. |

## Verdict: NOT-YET
A real worry on a weak evidence base. The MIT study is a small preprint with a rebuttal, and the surface area BOSS could touch (a "you're atrophying your judgment" nudge) is exactly where it would sound preachy and off-voice. The better-evidenced neighbors — the competence-gate ([[RVW-039]]) and the 70% problem ([[RVW-053]]) — already carry the actionable half ("stay able to grade the output"). Hold this until the finding replicates.

## If REJECT / NOT-YET
- **Why not:** Thin evidence (n=54 preprint + rebuttal); high preach-risk against BOSS voice; the actionable core is already carried by RVW-039/053.
- **Re-open condition:** A larger, replicated study (ideally on builders/founders, not essay-writers) confirms persistent skill erosion — then consider *one* conscience line, carefully voiced.
