# RVW-134 — every yes to a design-system request is a long-term commitment; price the work around the work

## The claim
- **Source:** a design director's essay on saying no, 2026-04.
- **Core assertion:** A component request costs far more than building it: edge cases, a11y, docs, onboarding, support, maintenance, forever. Evaluate the *total cost of supporting it*. Saying no transparently is stewardship, and fast yeses scale your pain.
