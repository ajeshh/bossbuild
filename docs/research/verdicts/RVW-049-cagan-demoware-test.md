# RVW-049 — the demoware test: "if it only replaces a prompt + copy/paste, it's a demo, not a product"

## The claim
- **Source:** Marty Cagan / SVPG, *AI Product Management 2 Years In* (Dec 2024 + 2025 follow-ups). Prototypes are for discovery, not delivery; fidelity should match risk; AI makes demoware trivially easy and easy to mistake for a validated product.
- **Core assertion:** AI lowers the cost of an impressive demo, so the discipline that matters is distinguishing demo from product — test value/viability/usability/feasibility, unchanged by AI.
