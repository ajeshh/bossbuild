# RVW-035 — psychological safety pairs with high standards, it isn't niceness (Edmondson, HBR 2025)

## The claim
- **Source:** Harvard Business Review, May–Jun 2025 (Edmondson & Kerrissey)
- **Core assertion:** Psychological safety is the condition where people can tell the truth — and must be paired with **high standards**, not traded against them.
- **Inbox file:** `docs/research/inbox/edmondson-psychological-safety-misunderstood.md`

## Verdict: NOT-YET
Sound, well-evidenced, and a real sharpening — but it lands on a cohort BOSS doesn't have. `mentor-talent` is V1 and correctly defaults to "not yet" at n=1. The honest move is to hold it against a **named, near-term trigger**: the live **IDEA-037 founding-teams** program has a "mentor-the-team" slice (slice 5) where coaching a real cofounder team across skill gaps is exactly this. That's where it earns its place — not as free-floating talent advice today.

## If REJECT / NOT-YET
- **Why not:** No live team cohort; `mentor-talent` defers at n=1 (#2).
- **Re-open condition:** IDEA-037 reaches the mentor-the-team slice (slice 5), OR a real founding team adopts BOSS — then ADAPT into that slice's coaching (safety + standards as one paired heuristic).
