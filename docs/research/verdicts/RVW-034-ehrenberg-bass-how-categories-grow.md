# RVW-034 — "penetration drives category growth early, price later" (Ehrenberg-Bass, JBR 2025)

## The claim
- **Source:** Journal of Business Research 2025 (S0148296325002085); Ehrenberg-Bass Institute
- **Core assertion:** Category growth is driven by **penetration early, price-per-volume later** — a replicated empirical law (13 yrs, 474 CPG categories).
- **Inbox file:** `docs/research/inbox/ehrenberg-bass-how-categories-grow.md`

## Rubric
| # | Question | Finding |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Contradicts a PRINCIPLE? | No — but #2 (JIT, never premature ceremony) is the live tension: this is a growth-stage concern. |
| 2 | Evidence grade | **The best-evidenced item in this sweep** — peer-reviewed, replicated, large panel. Ehrenberg-Bass is a source BOSS already respects (Byron Sharp / *How Brands Grow*). |
| 3 | Duplicate or sharpen? | Net-new to `mentor-gtm` — but the *transfer* from CPG to early-stage software/AI is **unproven** here. "Penetration before premium" is a brand-growth law; most BOSS founders are pre-PMF, not choosing category-growth strategy. |
| 4 | Who serves / harms? | Would serve a founder at the positioning/growth stage. Risk: handing penetration-vs-premium theory to a pre-PMF `first-product` founder is exactly the premature ceremony #2 refuses. |
| 5 | Cost / ceremony | Light as one heuristic — but premature for the cohort that's actually live. |

## Verdict: NOT-YET
The evidence is the strongest we've vetted, and rejecting it would be rejecting good science on a timing technicality — which is precisely what NOT-YET exists for. The killing issue isn't quality, it's **stage**: `mentor-gtm` founders are overwhelmingly pre-PMF, and the CPG→software transfer is unestablished. Hold it. When a real founder is at the category-positioning/growth stage (or `mentor-gtm` is being deepened with a growth-strategy layer), fold it in as a single attributed heuristic ("penetration before premium; build mental/physical availability") with the CPG-origin caveat stated.

## If REJECT / NOT-YET
- **Why not:** Premature for the live cohort (#2); CPG→software transfer unproven.
- **Re-open condition:** A founder reaches the growth/category-positioning stage, OR `mentor-gtm` gets a deliberate growth-strategy layer — then ADAPT as one attributed heuristic with the transfer caveat.
