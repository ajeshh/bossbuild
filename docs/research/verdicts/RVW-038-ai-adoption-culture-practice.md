# RVW-038 — a founder rolling AI out to a team without breeding resentment

## The claim
- **Source:** Stanford WORKBank / **Human Agency Scale** (arXiv 2506.06576, Jun 2025) + Edmondson psych-safety-under-AI (2025) + Mollick "Secret Cyborgs" (2024) + Workslop peer-trust erosion (HBR/BetterUp×Stanford, Sep 2025). Ledger: `sessions/SESSION-2026-06-20-research-feeds-mining.md`.
- **Core assertion:** Top-down/forced AI adoption breeds hiding, resentment, and "workslop." The humane move is **consent + psychological safety + leaders modeling honest use** — automate the drudgery workers *want* automated (the Human Agency Scale H1–H5), never the "Red Light" capable-but-unwanted work.

## Verdict: ADAPT
The single biggest net-new opportunity in this whole session, and the direct answer to the founder's ask ("how do I use AI without my team hating me for forcing it"). ADAPT not ADOPT because **BOSS's unit is a 2–5 person founding team, not a 10,000-person org** — strip the enterprise change-management framing, keep the team-scale spine: the **Human Agency Scale** (automate what's wanted, name the Red-Light zone), **psychological safety** (make it safe to admit "I don't know AI"), and **honest-use modeling** (kill the secret-cyborg dynamic), with **workslop trust-erosion** as the stakes. Suggestive, never a mandate (forcing humane-AI-adoption would be its own irony).
