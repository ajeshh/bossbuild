# RVW-047 — validate-first, stated honestly: faster *decisions*, not guaranteed wins

## The claim
- **Source:** Camuffo/Gambardella scientific-approach RCT (SMJ Jun 2024, 759 firms — theorize-then-test → terminate weak ideas, decisive pivots) + A/B-testing & startup performance (Mgmt Sci 2022, ~35k startups — experimentation makes you scale *and fail* faster) + theory-before-test pivots (Strategy Science 2024).
- **Core assertion:** Disciplined validation works largely by helping founders **decide faster — including quitting faster** — and cheap AI lowers the cost of *building*, not the cost of *being wrong*. "Validation guarantees you win" is not what the evidence says.
