# RVW-130 — a vendor survey: AI pays back in documentation, not generation; 59% see UI that bypasses the system

## The claim
- **Source:** a design-system vendor's practitioner survey summary, 2026-05.
- **Core assertion:** Of 123 practitioners, 82% use AI. Documentation is the most satisfying use (63%) and design generation the least (67%). Only 17% call their docs very AI-ready. 59% see UI bypassing the system. The blocker is time, not budget. *"The teams winning with AI are the ones who made their design systems legible to it."*
