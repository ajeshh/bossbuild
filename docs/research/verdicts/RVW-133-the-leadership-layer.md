# RVW-133 — design systems fail at the leadership layer: ownership, incentives, sponsorship

## The claim
- **Source:** a design-systems leader's essay, 2026-04.
- **Core assertion:** Systems fade rather than fail: teams route around them. The root causes are unclear ownership, incentives that reward shipping over reuse, and sponsorship that funds without backing. Leaders make it infrastructure by clarifying decision rights and refusing to absorb every exception.
- **Inbox file:** `~/Projects/inbox/bossbuild/Why most design systems fail at the leadership layer…pdf`
