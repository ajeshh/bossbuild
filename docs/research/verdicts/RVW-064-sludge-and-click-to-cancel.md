# RVW-064 — sludge / administrative-burden as a design weapon (+ the click-to-cancel rule's status)

## Verdict: NOT-YET
The *mechanics* of sludge are already in the catalog under Obstruction (RVW-056) — roach-motel and
hard-to-cancel are named, with "exit as easy as entry" as the humane alternative. What's **not** adopted is
sludge as a distinct *framing* (deliberate friction as a weapon; the "sludge audit" practice) and the
click-to-cancel regulatory hook — and the latter looks **vacated**, which is exactly why importing it now
would have been wrong. Hold for a dedicated pass.
