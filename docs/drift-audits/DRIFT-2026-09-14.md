# Drift audit — 2026-09-14

The first `/drift-deep` on BOSS's own tree (the RESUME carried *"never run here"* since the 09-12
retro). Read: the venture canvas's riskiest-assumption cell and its experiment line, all 24 devlog
entries, the 19 FEAT records, the three EVID records and the candidate list, IDEA-104 → IDEA-118, the

that bears on the bet; `docs/research/` was read for dates only.

## The named bet

- **Riskiest assumption** (verbatim, `docs/ideas/CANVAS.md`): *"Will a real founder **return** to BOSS,
  and will it change a decision they would otherwise have made worse? Everything else in this canvas
  is downstream of that. Today it is **n=0 observed**."*
- **Validation plan on record:** yes, and specific — *"Get BOSS in front of 1–3 real founders in the

  point outweighs any release on the backlog."* Phase 3 of it is prepared to the sentence:
  `docs/evidence/CANDIDATES-2026-08-23-maintainer-experiment.md` — three maintainers chosen, two
  messages drafted, `status: ready`, created **2026-08-23**. Twenty-two days ago.

## Verdict: drifting

The bet is stated precisely, the falsifier is cheap and named, and the plan to run it is written down
to the individual message — and the work of the last three weeks has been almost entirely the thing
the canvas says is downstream: what a founder would read back *after* returning. None of it is wrong,
and much of it is what a returning founder would need. But "building the thing they'd see when they
come back" is the sharpest drift shape this skill names: **building the very thing the risk says to
defer.** The memory note from 07-23 said it plainly — *re-aim, build nothing yet; when earned, compose
and subtract* — and the tree has composed and subtracted well (no new skill since; IDEA-118 removed
more lines than it added) while 270 commits landed on surfaces no stranger has opened.

The honest sentence for a returning founder: the body of work is at the level of conviction of
someone who believes the bet already, and the bet has not been asked once since August.

## The specific gaps

- **The experiment is written and not run.** `CANDIDATES-2026-08-23` has sat at `status: ready` for
  22 days in RESUME's *Waiting on Ajesh* with a 🔷. Every reader BOSS built this fortnight would show it
  as a Building card with 0/1 criteria ticked — if the experiment were a FEAT. It is not, so nothing on
  the board carries it.
- **The one instrument is on arrival, not return.** `copy_install` counts a stranger copying the
  command; nothing counts a second session. `boss insights` could — it reads `~/.boss/` per machine
  — but it reads Ajesh's machine only, by design.
- **The showcase answers a question the bet does not ask.** Kettlewick shows what BOSS *produces*; the
  bet is whether a founder *returns*. A stranger who opens the demo and leaves is a page view.
- **IDEA-109 (coach-in-residence) is a plan for n=0 that keeps n at 0** — it measured before opining
  and the next step is one call; that call is another one not yet made.

## Smallest re-aim

- **Send the first maintainer message** — the one already drafted in `CANDIDATES-2026-08-23`, the one
  that does not mention BOSS. Not `/canvas` (the experiment line is sharp) and not `/pretotype` (the fake
  door is parked on a trigger that this message would trip). One send. Then watch, don't ask.
