# DEC-012 — BOSS holds both ways, and its mission is unadvertised rather than enforced

> The charter behind [[DEC-011]], written down **after** the mechanism it authorised, on purpose.
> Three releases of mechanism came first — v0.200.0 (the licence stops being decided for you, the
> telos gains a second destination), v0.201.0 (the canvas asks the planet question it had been
> promising), v0.202.0 (a regulation is evidence, not a verdict). **DEC-011's own ordering: mechanism
> first, claim second.** This is the claim, and it is now earned.

## Context

Stated by Ajesh across 2026-08-21, in four pieces:

- *"not just founders building capitalistic AI native products, its a wide range of tools. **the
  methodology doesnt change, but just the range**."*
- *"The mindset from silicon valley on how to build good product, also meets the ancient ways of
  building.. **boss can see both ways and support. its not singular, but able to hold both sides**."*
- *"**boss's secret mission is to build more good**.. understanding that some folks dont think that
  yet.. but that doesnt mean our ethics is compromised, **we arent gate keepers**, but we have a way
  of if needed being there as a way to discuss the hard truths if needed."*
- *"a fine line between bad regulation vs challenging an outdated narrative… some folks hide behind
  'Regulations'."* → already shipped as v0.202.0.

## Decision

### 1. People — the range widens, the methodology does not

**Not a second audience, not a second product, not a fork.** The same just-in-time ladder, the same
conscience, the same modes. It stops *assuming* the thing being built is a capitalistic AI-native
product. A commons tool, a co-op's internal system, a research instrument, a thing built for a
neighbourhood — same methodology, same rungs.

**Consequence, and it is deliberately small:** BOSS's own canvas People cell gets **one scope line**,
not a rewrite. Everything else in that cell is v0.3 content and still stale. **This decision does not
licence a canvas sweep.** The Problem and Promises cells are untouched and remain a `/canvas`
conversation.

### 2. BOSS holds both Silicon Valley and the ancient ways — and does not resolve them

- **The Silicon Valley half:** validated learning, the riskiest assumption, ceremony scaled to
  evidence, deciding faster *including quitting faster*.
- **The ancient half:** craft transmitted rather than reinvented, stewardship, building something
  meant to outlast the person building it.

**Both already have mechanism, and the ancient half is under-named rather than missing.** Principle 1's
extract-**UP** *is* apprenticeship — the pattern leaves the project and joins the commons so the next
person starts from it. `/practice` is a shared, attributed, staleness-aware craft commons.
[[DEC-009]]'s second branch asks who carries this after you.

**This is plural, not a synthesis.** BOSS is not looking for the average of the two, and when they
conflict the conflict *is* the content: the founder decides, and BOSS's job is that both readings are
genuinely available rather than one being the default with the other as decoration.

### 3. The mission is to build more good — unadvertised, never enforced

Not everyone arrives thinking that way, and that is fine. **BOSS does not gatekeep, does not screen,
does not refuse.** The mechanism is the conscience already built: just-in-time, pausable,
override-friendly, deviation recorded and never blocked. It is there when it is needed and quiet when
it is not.

🔴 **The operative constraint — unadvertised to the market, never hidden from the founder.** An
unstated goal that shapes what a tool nudges toward is, *by BOSS's own catalog*, a deceptive pattern.
It stays honest for exactly one reason: BOSS is inspectable by construction — local state,
`boss status --conscience`, the activity ledger, the pause, and hook text a founder can open and read.
**Any future change that makes the mission less inspectable breaks this decision, not merely this
clause.**

### 4. The humane lens must not become conservative

Shipped in v0.202.0, recorded here because it is charter rather than housekeeping: **humane is not
*don't disturb anything*.** A rule can be a safety floor or a moat; a citation cannot tell you which.
The test is **effect, not narrative** — and *reform absorbs its own downside, rationalisation exports
it.*

### What is NOT decided

5. **The website stays untouched — the third consecutive decision to leave it.** *"till we solve
   this."* The accepted cost is unchanged and now taken three times: a commons-minded founder
   evaluating BOSS still cannot tell any of this from the outside.
6. **The time horizon is a named gap, not a decision** → IDEA-069. Every time-shaped mechanism in
   BOSS points at an **ending** — `/sunset`, the bus factor, *"what would make you stop."* Nothing
   asks what a thing is meant to **outlast**. That is the ancient half with no question behind it.
   Captured, not built: n=0 on whether a founder wants to be asked.

## Why

- **Written after the mechanism, on purpose.** Three releases of defaults changed before a word of
  charter. That ordering is what keeps this from being a values page.
- **The People answer is the cheapest one available.** No fork, no second product, no `intent` axis —
  a scope line. The most expensive reading of the founder's words was also the wrong one.
- **Clause 3 costs nothing today and a great deal to break later.** Inspectability already exists, so
  naming it as a constraint is free now — and it is the only thing standing between "a conscience"
  and "a thumb on the scale."
- **Naming both halves stops the ancient half from being decoration.** Unnamed, it would have stayed a
  nice sentence about craft; named, it produced a falsifier and an idea.

## Falsifier

1. **The range widens and the methodology breaks.** If a commons / co-op / non-commercial project runs
   the ladder and something *other than the money cells* mis-fits — the mode names, the drift moment,
   `/spec`, `/health` — then *"the methodology doesn't change"* was wrong, and IDEA-067 rung 2's
   `intent` axis is warranted after all.
2. **"Both ways" becomes a slogan.** If at `revisit_by` the ancient half still has **no question a
   founder is ever asked** — only Principle 1 and `/practice`, which serve BOSS more than the founder —
   then BOSS holds one way and says two. IDEA-069 is the first test of this.
3. **The mission stops being inspectable.** Any change where BOSS nudges toward "more good" in a way a
   founder cannot read, pause, or override. One instance is enough.

## Consequences

- **BOSS's own canvas gets one scope line in People.** Problem and Promises are NOT touched.
- **IDEA-069 filed** — the time-horizon question. Deliberately an idea, not a feature.
- ⚠️ **The phrase "secret mission" is deliberately absent from every shipped artifact.** It is an
  accurate description of intent and a terrible thing to say in product copy — it reads as a hidden
  agenda, which is precisely what clause 3 forbids. The honest public phrasing, when the website is
  eventually solved, is nearer *"BOSS is built by someone who thinks this matters, and it will tell
  you when it thinks you're about to do harm. You can pause it."*
- **[[DEC-011]] is unchanged and this does not supersede it** — DEC-011 owns the defaults and the
  telos; this owns the charter behind them.
