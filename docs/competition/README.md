## What this field says, read together

**Five rows now, and the last two change the picture.** The founder-shaped plugin niche *exists*
and is a graveyard: every plugin that says *founder* or *startup idea* is at 0–33 stars, several
were abandoned the day they were made, and the most ambitious retired itself. All of them are
one-shot pipelines — idea → verdict → spec → nothing. Next door, the craft niche has a 285k-star
default that never asks *why*. **BOSS's bet — that the value is in staying (modes, re-entry, an
evidence ledger the conscience reads) — is the one shape nobody in the field tried.** That is either
the gap or the reason they left, and n=0 cannot yet say which. Two things to study, not copy:
product-discovery's *synthetic* evidence rung, and superpowers' one-repo-fourteen-hosts layout.


**Three rows now, and the third sits *inside* the first.** `frontend-design` is what "doing nothing"
looks like when the host's maker adds one file — the first screen gets genuinely better, and nothing
survives to the second session. It is not a rival to BOSS's records or conscience; it is a layer BOSS
lacks (a current, dated list of what generated design looks like *right now*) that lacks every layer
BOSS has. **The one thing to act on is that its list is from this month and BOSS's is from 2024.**


Two rows, and they bracket BOSS exactly. **Doing nothing** is where every founder starts and is
what BOSS is actually measured against — EVID-001 and EVID-003 are both people who tried raw Claude
Code first. **v0** is what a founder reaches for when they want the *thing on the internet today*,
and it has no answer to *why did we build this* or *where are we* — the two questions BOSS exists
for. The design-system overlap is real and worth watching; the conscience and the records have no
rival in this table yet.
