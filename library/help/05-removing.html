Scaffolding comes down when it is holding nothing up. boss remove previews exactly what would go and changes nothing; boss remove --apply does it, after copying .boss/ aside first.

Your records are yours. Ideas, decisions, evidence, the devlog — everything you wrote stays. What comes out is BOSS's own machinery.

Files you wrote yourself are tracked separately

If you brought BOSS into a repo that already had its own .claude/agents/ or skills, those are marked unclaimed — BOSS knows it did not write them. They are not overwritten on day one, they are skipped by boss sync --apply --keep-mine on day thirty, and they are not removed when BOSS leaves. A file BOSS never wrote is not BOSS's to delete.

Staying current is a reviewed diff, never a silent update

boss sync shows you what changed in BOSS since your project's pin and what it would do about it; --apply is a separate decision. Nothing about your project changes because something changed upstream.
