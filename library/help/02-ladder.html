“Scaffolding” here is the construction word, used literally: the temporary structure that goes up around a building while it is being built. You do not live in it. It holds things steady, it gets added to as the building gets taller, and it comes down when it is no longer holding anything up.

So BOSS arrives one rung at a time. A funded company gets structure from a process, a board and a team; solo with an AI you get none of it — but a one-evening idea needs almost none either. You never pay for ceremony the project has not earned.

What each rung is for

  • Quickstart — get the idea out of your head. Capture freely, pressure-test one, talk to one real person, and test demand before building. Nothing about specs, tests or deploys exists here.
  • MVP — build the first working version. The spec discipline and the smoke gate: the first rung where “is it alive?” is a gate rather than a habit. Design tokens land the moment there is real UI.
  • V1 — make it shippable. Real users are hitting it, so the questions turn outward: is what I made working for the people using it?
  • Scale — grow it. Customers are real and coordination is the bottleneck.

Each rung names its own price before you climb

boss unlock <mode> tells you what that rung pays for itself on — and then declines to stop you. Some conditions BOSS can check against your files; the rest are yours to judge, and it says which are which. Climbing without them is allowed and the deviation is yours to own; you will just be carrying ceremony you have not earned yet.

A project that stays in Quickstart forever is a legitimate outcome, not a failure. Most ideas should die there, cheaply.
