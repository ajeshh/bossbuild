# `/scout customer` — what people already say about this problem

**This is not evidence, and the difference matters.** A stranger's forum post is someone talking to
the internet, not to you; it can't be graded on the evidence ladder (*said · did · committed*) and it
never becomes an `EVID`. What it is good for: finding the words people use, the workarounds they've
built, and **who to go and talk to** — `/interview` is the next step this domain points at, almost
every time.

- **Complaints over praise.** What people praise tells you what exists; what they complain about
  tells you where the field is weak. Read praise only to learn what users have come to expect.
- **Workarounds are the strongest signal desk research has.** A spreadsheet someone built, a script
  shared in a thread, a paid assistant doing it by hand — a workaround is a need with effort already
  spent on it. Record what they built, not just that they're annoyed.
- **Count people, not posts.** Ten replies from one person is one person.
- **Persona feed:** findings here go into the persona's *synthetic* share, never its *real* share —
  only someone who spoke to you moves that (`/persona`).
