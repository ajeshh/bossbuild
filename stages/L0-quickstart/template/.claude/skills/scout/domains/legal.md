# `/scout legal` — what the rules say

**This is research, never legal advice.** It tells you what a text says, where it lives and when it
was last changed, so the conversation with a lawyer is short and cheap. Say *"check this with a lawyer
before relying on it"* whenever money, liability or someone's personal data depends on the answer —
as part of the answer, not as a footnote.

- **`regulation <area>`** — which rules plausibly apply (by what you do, where your users are, what
  data you hold), the text of each, and the regulator's own guidance. Name what you could not
  determine — *"depends on whether you count as a processor"* is a finding.
- **`name <name>`** — the official trademark register for the classes you'd sell in, the domain, the
  handles you'd need. A search result is not a clearance; say so.
- **`terms <platform>`** — the current terms of the app store, API or marketplace you're building on:
  what they can change, what they forbid, what they take. Date it; these move.
- **Hand-offs:** privacy and the trust page → `/trust` (MVP); a decision you take on the strength of
  this → `/decide`, with the text it rests on cited.
