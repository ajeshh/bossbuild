# `/scout people` — who knows this, and who's hiring for it

- **`experts <topic>`** — find people by their **work**, not their reach: who measured the thing, who
  shipped it, whose claim held up when someone tried to break it. List the established names *and*
  the ones doing the newest measured work; a new voice earns a place by being right once, not by
  being known. Each person: what they've done on this topic, with links and dates, and what of
  theirs you've verified. **No scores and no ranking of people** — an order to read them in, with the
  reason.
- **`roles <role>`** — what the role is called in the market, what it pays (from sources that publish
  their method), where those people are found. Lands in `docs/research/people/`.
- **Never about a private person.** Candidates, references, resumes and anyone who hasn't put their
  work in public stay out of research files entirely; a resume someone sent you stays in
  `docs/source/`, labelled `kind: hr`.
