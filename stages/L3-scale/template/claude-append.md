## Scale working rules (added on `boss unlock scale`)

> {{MODE}} mode: the product has customers, the founder has help, and **coordination — not code — is
> the bottleneck.** Your job is now **leader** (give away your Legos). Everything here is
> **symptom-gated**; premature ceremony is the disease BOSS treats.

1. **High-risk paths get the human tier.** Name the paths where a mistake is expensive and hard to
   reverse — **schema/migrations, billing, auth** — in this file as they emerge. Changes there need the
   *other human* + `/smoke` + `/evals` before merge (`git-workflow`'s risk tiers, one named list).
2. **Decisions get an owner.** `/decide` carries an optional `dri:`; recording an owner ≠ gating.
3. **The customer loop is the signal.** `/idea --feedback` registers it (a feature request is a
   stated-pain `EVID`, never a spec); `/incident` runs the blameless post-mortem — fix first, one
   systemic learning to `/extract`.
4. **The conscience never fires at, evaluates, or reports on a non-founder.** When a roster grows, the
   conscience still coaches *the founder* only. It does not grade contractors or employees, does not
   surface per-person output, and never becomes a management surveillance tool. This is a hard line,
   not a setting.
5. **The conscience matures by register, not new hooks** — `focus` reads the team's WIP, `drift` reads
   strategy. Earlier moments keep firing.

**Scale refuses**, until a real symptom re-opens it: product councils and PM-org cosplay; `RFC-NNN`
before two builders disagree in writing (`EXP`/`AUDIT` likewise); calendar reflection ceremonies
(`/close` carries reflection).

## What Scale adds (alongside V1)

**Run `boss map`** for the rung's skills, live from the install: `/incident` (the blameless one-page
post-mortem) and `/idea --feedback` (the customer register, writing into the evidence ledger). One
mentor arrives: **`mentor-hiring`** — first hires, contractors vs employees, what to keep vs delegate.
It **defaults to *don't hire yet***, so ask it before you post a role; advisory only, never binding
employment, equity or labour advice. Everything else waits on a real project's symptom.
