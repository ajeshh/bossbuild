Product leader. Innovation catalyst. Builder of thriving systems.

▸ajesh shah

Ajesh Shah has spent a decade as a product leader in complex, cross-functional environments — fintech, e-commerce, enterprise retail, education and social impact — usually brought in to build trust quickly and get very different teams aligned enough to move. That work included 20% revenue growth at Macy's, 12% conversion gains at Happy Money, and architecting the financial engine behind MoveHQ's multibillion-dollar logistics platform. Alongside it he has taught product management, led Lean methodology workshops and conferences, designed and run hackathons, and built large-scale art.

As Community Architect for Burning Man's Temple projects he grew a volunteer base from 300 to 1,600 and built the mentorship and ownership systems that made it work. Nobody there was paid and nobody could be managed, so the only thing that scaled was structure that let other people do good work and know where they stood — put up when it was needed, taken down when it wasn't. BOSS is that idea as a tool, which is why its sixth principle is humane before viable and why it gets quieter as real evidence arrives rather than louder.
