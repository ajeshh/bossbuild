▸ajesh shah

Ajesh Shah has spent a decade as a product leader in companies ranging from a handful of people to the Fortune 500 — fintech, e-commerce, enterprise retail, education and social impact. The work has mostly been the same shape wherever it happened: walk into something complicated, build trust quickly, and get very different people aligned enough to build the thing that looked impossible. Together, which is the part that makes it hold.

Scaffolding is the method underneath that, and it took a long time to get right — sharpened by teaching product management, running Lean methodology workshops and conferences, designing hackathons, and building large-scale art. Structure that goes up when it helps, holds steady while the real thing takes shape, and comes down afterwards. BOSS is that practice as a tool, which is why its sixth principle is humane before viable and why it gets quieter as real evidence arrives rather than louder.

The Humane Product Canvas came out of the same practice, and is free to take. Ajesh is on LinkedIn and GitHub; the decisions behind BOSS are their own page.
