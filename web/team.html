The team

You're one person. BOSS staffs the project.

A funded company puts a team around the build: someone deciding what's worth making, someone testing it, designers, a database person — and a board to tell the founder the truth. Solo with an AI you get none of that, so you either invent it every project or skip it and find out later. BOSS brings {{COUNT_AGENTS}} of them, in two classes, and hires them just in time — never all at once.

▸two classes
{{COUNT_BUILDERS}} builders — they make the product A product lead, coders, a tester, a program manager, designers, a database architect. They do work: write the spec, write the code, catch the regression, review the screen.
{{COUNT_MENTORS}} mentors — they coach you Advisory only. They never write product code. They pressure-test the bet, name the riskiest assumption, and tell you when the honest answer is “not yet.” This class is the part almost nothing else ships.

The difference matters: a builder makes the thing better, a mentor makes your decision better. Most tools give you the first kind and call it a team.

▸the ladder

They arrive when the project earns them

A one-evening idea does not need a database architect, and a product with real users should not be missing one. So the roster grows with the work — you unlock each rung, nothing is ever taken away, and a project that stays in Quickstart forever is a legitimate project.
