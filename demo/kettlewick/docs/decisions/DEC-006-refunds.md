# DEC-006 — The first month back, no questions

## Context
One agency has paid (EVID-004) and three are about to be asked for October by payment link
(MONEY-2026-09-20). Nobody has asked for money back yet, which is the time to decide what happens
when someone does: calmly, before it's a phone call.

## Decision
The first month comes back on request within 30 days, no questions asked and no form. After that, an
agency can cancel any time in one tap (FEAT-006), and the unused part of the month comes back
with it.

## Why
Considered: no refunds, since it's £4 a carer. Chosen: the first month back. Why not none: the owners
talk to each other, and a story about a small agency that couldn't get £28 back would travel through
the owners' group faster than any fill. Considered: a pro-rata refund from day one. Why not: it puts
arithmetic in front of an owner who just wants to leave.

## Falsifier — what would prove this wrong, and by when?
If more than one in five paying agencies asks for the first month back by 2026-12-01, the price or
the product is wrong, not this rule. Look there first.

## Consequences
The payment link's page says it in one line under the price. FEAT-003's card form inherits it.
