# DEC-002 — Phone-first, and the schedule stays a spreadsheet

## Context
Four owners covered from a phone in June. The office screen was where Shiftwise lives, not where Monday happens.

## Decision
The cover flow is phone-first; the office screen is the same page, wider. The schedule stays the owner's spreadsheet; Kettlewick reads it and never edits it.

## Why
Considered: build a schedule too (Shiftwise's ground, a year of work); chosen: cover only; why not: every owner said the schedule is fine and the cover is the pain.

## Falsifier — what would prove this wrong, and by when?
If half the fills by December start from the office screen, the phone bet was wrong.

## Consequences
No scheduling features. Owners keep two tools, and that is the point.
