# DEC-003 — No location tracking, no league table, nothing after 8pm

## Context
Two owners asked to see where carers are; one asked who says yes most.

## Decision
Kettlewick never reads a carer's location, never ranks carers, and sends nothing between 8pm and 6:30am.

## Why
Considered: opt-in tracking; chosen: none; why not: a carer who is watched is a carer who leaves, and the owners' own words say they'd abandon a tool that does it.

## Falsifier — what would prove this wrong, and by when?
If owners leave for a rival because of tracking, by July 2027, the cost was real. We'd still not add it.

## Consequences
A feature two owners asked for is refused on the page they can read.
