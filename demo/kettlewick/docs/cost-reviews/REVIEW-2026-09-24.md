# AI cost review — Kettlewick — 2026-09-24

## Headline
Two and a half US cents for the whole window. Every agency is far under budget. The one thing worth
fixing is that a timed-out call never reaches the ledger.

## Numbers
- **Window:** 2026-09-15 to 2026-09-24 (live asks since 2026-09-19)
- **Total spend:** $0.0252 (35 calls, 9 agencies plus dev and eval, 1 FEAT)
  - live asks: $0.0152, 21 calls across all nine agencies
  - eval run (2026-09-18, five cases twice): $0.0072, 10 calls
  - Ola's dev calls (2026-09-15/16, `userId` null): $0.0028, 4 calls
- **Per draft:** $0.00070 median, $0.00116 the most (the call cut off at the output cap, below)
- **Per agency/day:** the busiest was `ag_04` on Monday 2026-09-22: two asks, $0.0014, against a
  $0.05 budget (3%). Median agency-day with any ask: $0.0007.
- **Monthly run-rate:** about 3.7 live asks a day → about 110 a month → **about $0.08 a month**,
  against a $10 cap.
- **By FEAT:** FEAT-007 $0.0252 (100%, the only call site)
- **By model:** `small-fast` $0.0252 (100%)

## Gross margin
- **Cost per active agency:** about $0.009 a month for the model (under 1p). Add the texts and hosting
  (about 40p a carer, so £3.60 for a nine-carer agency) and the whole cost is about £3.61.
- **Price/ARPU:** one agency paying, £36 a month for nine carers (EVID-004). The other eight are in the free first month.
- **Gross margin:** about 90% on the paying agency. The model is 0.02% of what it pays; the texts are
  the other 10%. No agency is near the margin-trap line (cost over 10–15% of ARPU).
- **Evergreen ratio (cached ÷ total input):** 0%. The fixed prompt is about 380 tokens, under the
  provider's cache minimum. It's a non-lever at this size, not a missed win.
- **Struggle check:** 0% of spend is retries. There is no "write it again" (cut in FEAT-007), and
  the one garbage retry the handler allows never fired this week.

## Variance against budget
- Per agency per day: under. Highest $0.0014 against $0.05.
- Per agency per month: under. On pace for about $0.009 against $0.50.
- Monthly cap: under. About $0.08 projected against $10.
- Per-call caps: one output-cap hit in 21 live calls. No input-cap hits.
- Model rationale: still true. One model, one call site, latency is the reason, and the evals pass on it.

## Surprises
- **A timed-out call writes no line.** On 2026-09-22 at 07:41 one ask hit the 4-second timeout and
  went out as the template, as declared. The ledger doesn't show it, because the logger runs after
  a reply and a timeout has none. The app's own log is the only record, so a run of timeouts
  would look like a quiet week here.
- **One reply ran to the 200-token cap** (`ag_07`, 2026-09-22 10:05). The cut-off JSON failed the
  schema check, and the template went out. The handler did its job. The cause isn't visible from
  the ledger (token counts only, by design), so it's now an eval case (`feat-007-fail-008`) that
  asserts the cut-off reply falls back.
- **Eval and dev traffic was 40% of the window's spend.** Expected in the ship week; it will shrink
  as live asks grow. Not a finding.
