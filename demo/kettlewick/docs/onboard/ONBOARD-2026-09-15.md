# Onboarding — 2026-09-15

**n = 9 owners. No funnel.** Under ten, an activation rate is nine anecdotes with a percent sign on
them. So this is the seam the step asks for at this size: what the owners said and where they
stalled in their first session, in their words, written down before anyone forgets what a first
session looked like. Then the plan for doing the next ones by hand.

## Where this sits on the journey
*Try it* and *First value* (`docs/product/JOURNEY.md`). The stage before sets the expectation: an
owner arrives from another owner's mention, expecting "the thing that finds cover", not software. The
stage after is the orphan: *Come back* has no flow, and the pretotype on 2026-09-14 (EVID-005) showed
one owner skipping it for a reason, not by forgetting.

## First sessions — their words
From Marta's notes on the nine first sessions (2026-08-28 to 2026-09-10), and the two-week
check-ins. Owners are fictional; their words are kept as Marta wrote them down.

- *"Does it change my sheet?"* — asked first by four of nine, before anything else. The answer
  (no, never) is the first thing the check screen should say, not the last.
- *"It's got Dee down as Tuesdays. She's not Tuesdays."* — the paste read a column wrong for one
  carer. The owner stopped trusting the rest of the parse and checked every row by hand (journey,
  *Try it*, observed).
- *"So which three will it pick?"* — three owners. Answered by showing, not by explaining: the row
  says "Asked Priya, Sam, Jo" after the tap.
- *"Nothing's uncovered today, so… what do I do?"* — three owners started on a day with nothing
  uncovered. The empty day said "Nothing uncovered today." and nothing else.
- *"I'll text them after so they know it's me."* — five of nine at the two-week check-ins. That
  became FEAT-007.

## The aha, as far as nine can say it
The six owners who filled a second Monday all **covered a visit in their first week**. Of the three
who filled once and not again, two had their first session on a day with nothing uncovered and first
covered a visit days later. That is a pattern in nine, not a finding: the candidate aha is **a visit
covered by Kettlewick, confirmed by the owner, without a call**. It is the activation metric
`MEASURE-2026-08-30` already named, so `/health` keeps tracking the same number.

## Time to value
Today: sign-up → paste the week → check the rows → add carers' days → wait for an uncovered visit.
The last step is the one nobody can design away, and it can take a week.

- **Cut:** the carers' days as a separate step. The paste already has who works when; ask only about
  the carers the paste couldn't place.
- **Cut:** the "check every row" screen as a wall. Show the rows the parse was unsure of first;
  the rest collapse to "28 rows read".
- **Keep:** the owner confirms every fill. It is a step, and it is the product.

## The first run
No sample data and no demo visit: a fake uncovered visit would send a real text to a real carer.
The honest version is the empty day saying what to do next: "Nothing uncovered today. When a carer
can't make it, tap the visit and Ask." That is `EmptyState`'s job, and the wording above is the
proposed change.

## Concierge plan — the next owners
The next owners come from the county owners' group, unasked (IDEA-006). For each:
- Marta sits with them, at their kitchen table or on a call, **on a Monday morning**, so the first
  session has an uncovered visit in it.
- She pastes their sheet with them and fixes the first wrong row in front of them.
- She writes down what they say in the first ten minutes, here, in their words, before their second
  session makes them someone who has seen it before.

## Refused
No setup checklist with a progress bar, no "complete your profile", no tour before the day view. An
owner who covered one visit and left satisfied is fully onboarded.
