# QuietNotice

**Why it exists:** new — a line on the owner's day view after 8pm saying when the queued asks will go out ("3 asks go out at 7am"). No existing component says *when* something will happen; the disabled AskCard says it to the carer, and the owner has nothing.

## When it applies
- the owner's day view between 8pm and 7am, when at least one ask is queued

## When it doesn't
- before 8pm — nothing is queued; the row's chip says *asked*
- as a toast — it is a standing fact, not a result
