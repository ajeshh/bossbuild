# PrintSheet

**Why it exists:** new — the printed Monday for the one owner in eight who works from paper; the only surface with no interaction.

## When it applies
- print, from the day view; one page, time order, one colour

## When it doesn't
- the screen — it is never rendered on screen except as the print preview

## Variants, and when
| Variant | Use it for | Never for |
|---|---|---|
| (none) | one sheet | a colour version |

## Content
- the day's visits with the three chip shapes; the two acts (*ring* and *cover by*) at equal weight — the exception of 2026-08-28, kept

## Layout
- A4 and Letter; 6mm margins; the mark at 6mm minimum; a raw hex for the rule colour — the exception of 2026-09-08, until the print family has tokens

## Accessibility
- one colour, shape carries state; 11pt minimum
- *not checked:* a monochrome laser at draft quality
