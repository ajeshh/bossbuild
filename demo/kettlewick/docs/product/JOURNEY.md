# The journey — Kettlewick

> The tier above the flows: the stages an owner travels, what they meet at each, and where they
> leave. Every row says where it came from — observed, said, or assumed.

| Stage | Trying to do | Meets | Serving flow | Where they leave | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## The gaps

- *Come back* has no flow and no FEAT — nothing brings an owner back on Monday except the dread; a Sunday-evening line ("3 visits uncovered tomorrow") is queued by the 8pm rule and needs PAT-4's *say when*
- the carer who won't install anything — one owner in nine; no flow serves the phone-only carer
