# Canvas — Kettlewick

## 1 · Human Foundation

| Cell | Answer |
|---|---|
| **People** | Owner-operators of small home-care agencies — 3 to 15 carers, run from a kitchen table or a back office. **About 6,400** such agencies in England (register extract, 2026-06-02, docs/source). We know from nine of them, in person. |
| **Problem** | Every Monday morning — and every Sunday night — the owner rebuilds cover by phone. One sick text means eight calls. The visit is the thing that can't slip; the owner is the only one who knows who can take it. |
| **Promises** | We find cover before the kettle boils. One tap; the right three carers get the ask; the first yes fills the visit; the owner sees it filled. |

## 2 · Product Expression

| Cell | Answer |
|---|---|
| **Story** | 7:40 on a Monday. A text: "can't do Mrs H at 9". The owner taps the visit, Kettlewick asks the three carers who are free, qualified and close; Priya says yes at 7:43; the owner is already making tea. What changed: the rota went to phones in 2024–25, the cover call never did. Smallest workflow: the ask, the yes, the fill. |
| **Modes of Engagement** | Self-serve, phone-first, and quiet: nothing pings after 8pm, no location tracking, no league table of who says yes. The owner's own carers are the only people asked. Why us: Marta ran a twelve-carer agency for six years and did this call every week; the product is her Monday, not a demo of one. |
| **Business Model** | £4 per carer per month, flat, paid by the agency; free for the carers. The first 100 find us through the county owners' group and the register (owners talk to owners); usage compounds because every fill is a text a carer keeps. Channels that keep working after the first 100: the owners' group, and the carers who move agencies. |
| **Cost Structure** _(live once there's a price, or a real cost)_ | About 40p per carer per month at nine agencies — texts and hosting; no model in the loop. Fixed: one laptop, one phone number. Runway: Ola part-time on savings until December; Marta full-time on the agency sale. If cover isn't earning by then, it's a hobby. |
| **What it takes to deliver** _(live when the answer isn't "just me and a laptop")_ | A phone number that sends texts reliably at 7 a.m.; the owner's rota as a CSV every week; Ola's evenings. Nothing else is load-bearing. |
| **Key Partnerships** _(live only if someone else is load-bearing)_ | None load-bearing. The register is open data; the text provider is swappable in a day. |

## 3 · Stewardship

| Cell | Answer |
|---|---|
| **Metrics** | It worked = five agencies we don't know run their Monday on it. Real pull: a visit filled by Kettlewick without the owner's phone (activation), and the same owner filling again the second Monday (retention). Nine owners on; six filled twice. |
| **Risks & Harms** | A visit marked covered that isn't — a person doesn't get their care. Carers pressured to say yes at 7 a.m. — refused by design (no reply is fine, nothing shows who declined). Location tracking — refused. The regulator names a duty of care to the person visited; our stance: the owner confirms every fill, Kettlewick never marks a visit covered on its own. |
| **Build or buy?** | Build the cover flow only. Buy the texting; keep the rota where it is (the spreadsheet). Rotawise does rotas; we don't. |
| **Principles** | Never sell carer data. Nothing after 8pm. The owner is the last word on every fill. |
