# Kettlewick

## Current shape
_The best articulation so far._
- **What:** One tap when a carer calls in sick. The schedule stays a spreadsheet; Kettlewick only does the cover.
- **Who it's for:** owner-operators with 3 to 15 carers who do the Monday schedule by hand.
- **Why now:** owners moved the schedule to their phones in 2024–25; the cover call didn't follow.
- **Smallest version that proves it:** a text goes out to the three carers who can take the visit; the first yes fills it; the owner sees it filled.

## Capture log
- 2026-05-12 — seed: "every Monday I lose an hour to the cover call. I want the hour back."
- 2026-05-20 — from the first three calls: nobody wants a new schedule. They want the *cover* done.
- 2026-06-02 — imported the register extract: about 6,400 small agencies in England (docs/source).
- 2026-07-15 — owner survey notes: the phone tree is the job they hate most; the spreadsheet they'd keep.
- 2026-08-28 — shipped to the first nine owners. Marta covered a Monday visit from the school gate.
