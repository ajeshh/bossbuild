# The phone tree

**What it is:** doing nothing — the owner rings carers one by one until someone says yes.

## Why they might win
It works today, it costs no money, and every owner knows how to do it.

## Where it breaks
- an hour a Monday, eight calls, and the owner is the single point of failure
- carers get rung at 10pm
