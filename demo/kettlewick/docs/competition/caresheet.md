# CareSheet

**What it is:** a shared spreadsheet template with a texting add-on; free, plus the cost of texts.

## Why they might win
It is the spreadsheet the owner already has, and free beats £4.

## Where it breaks
- the add-on texts every carer, every time — owners turn it off after the first complaint
- no yes: the owner still rings to confirm who's taking it

## How they do it
- rota: the owner's own sheet
- cover: broadcast text to all
- pricing: free; texts at cost
