## Verdict: mixed
What's solid: the product owners use is the product they'd pay for, and the work has stayed inside
cover. Nothing here built the schedule DEC-002 says not to. But from 2026-09-05 to 2026-09-21 the
bet's own experiment waited while the biggest build since August went to FEAT-007, which makes the
product better for owners already using it free and says nothing about whether the next one pays.
The test went out yesterday. Roughly one part in six of the period's work tested the bet.

## The specific gaps
- **FEAT-007 built around the bet.** Two weeks of evenings on the voice of the ask, while the
  heartbeat's line ("the next four haven't been asked") stayed true. It answered a real thing owners
  said; it was not the riskiest thing.
- **FEAT-003 was being built before the question it serves was answered.** The money record caught
  it: a billing system before three customers. It is parked behind the links now.
- **The canvas heartbeat still names the card form.** The plan moved to links on 09-20 and the canvas
  didn't.
