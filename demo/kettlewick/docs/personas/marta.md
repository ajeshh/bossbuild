# Persona — Marta, the owner-operator

**who** — 52, owns a nine-carer agency in a market town; runs it from the kitchen table before the school run, and from the school gate after it.
**context** — Sunday 8:40pm or Monday 7:40am: a text says "can't do tomorrow" and the phone tree starts — eight calls, an hour, a spreadsheet open on the laptop and a biro on the back of an envelope.
**jobs**
- keep every visit covered — a missed visit is a person without care, and a call from their daughter
- keep the carers she has; she can't hire her way out of a bad Monday
- keep the inspector happy without a second job of paperwork
- know, by the time the kettle boils, that Monday is sorted
**pains**
- the cover call takes an hour and eight calls, and the eighth is the one who says yes
- she's the only one who knows who can take what; if she's ill, nobody is covered
- every rota tool she's tried wanted her to move the rota into it — she won't
- asking at night feels like begging, and she does it anyway
**values**
- trusts a tool that does one thing and stays out of the way
- abandons anything that tracks her carers, ranks them, or pings them at night
- the carer's word over the system's guess
- the spreadsheet stays — it's hers, and it works
**what we DON'T know yet**
- whether she'd pay before the first missed visit, or only after
- whether her carers keep the texts, or delete them
- what she does when nobody says yes — does she go herself?
- whether "the right three" is a rule she'd let the app apply, or one she'd override every time
