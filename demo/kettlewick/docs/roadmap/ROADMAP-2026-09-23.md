# Roadmap — 2026-09-23

**n = 9 owners, pre-PMF** (HEALTH-2026-09-07; DRIFT-2026-09-22). Under ten there is nothing to weigh
in the usual sense, so the roadmap is one item: **find fit.** The three bets below are that one item,
in three pieces with a fixed appetite each, and two of the three are conversations, not builds. The
NO-list is the half that matters this cycle: three requests were waiting, and they're answered there.

The carers who answer the asks (about seventy) are users too, but none of them chose Kettlewick, so
they don't count toward fit. They count toward harm, which is DEC-003's job.

## Signal, kept apart
- **What owners do:** six of nine covered a second Monday; one paid before a form existed (EVID-004);
  one of three covered after a hand-sent nudge, and one had covered by phone on Sunday night because
  the ask would have waited (EVID-005); in FEAT-007's first week owners sent 14 of 20 drafts as
  written.
- **What owners say:** a schedule builder (two), a carer app (every owner, and one carer by reply to an ask, IDEA-002), an invoice
  export (two, IDEA-003), "just send the draft" (one), a referral button (none: referrals happen
  without one, IDEA-006).
