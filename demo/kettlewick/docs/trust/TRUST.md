# Trust — Kettlewick

We hold the schedule you give us and the phone numbers on it, minus the addresses and notes, which we skip; nothing else. Carers' locations are never read. Since September a model drafts the ask to your carers: it gets the day, the time, the area and three first names, never a client's name, address or care note. Nothing is sold, and nothing is used to train anything. Delete your agency and it is gone in a day, texts included. To ask about your data, text the number you already have.

- Who else handles it, and exactly what each gets: [SUBPROCESSORS.md](SUBPROCESSORS.md)
- Security, plainly: the app runs on one hosting provider in a UK region; two people have access, Marta and Ola, each signing in with a second factor. Nothing is certified yet. If an agency's contract needs a certificate, that is when we will start one.
